# Day 1 – AI Fundamentals

## 1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples:
- Understanding human language
- Recognizing images
- Making predictions
- Solving problems
- Decision making
- Learning from data


## 2. What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of Artificial Intelligence.

It allows computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every task.

Examples:
- Spam email detection
- Movie recommendation
- Fraud detection
- House price prediction


## 3. What is Deep Learning?

Deep Learning (DL) is a subset of Machine Learning.

It uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

Examples:
- Face recognition
- Speech recognition
- Self-driving technology
- Image classification


## 4. AI vs ML vs DL

### Artificial Intelligence
AI is the broad field of making machines intelligent.

### Machine Learning
ML is a part of AI that allows machines to learn from data.

### Deep Learning
DL is a part of ML that uses multi-layer neural networks.

Relationship:

AI
|
└── Machine Learning
    |
    └── Deep Learning


## 5. Real-World Applications of AI

### Healthcare
AI can help with disease prediction, medical image analysis and patient monitoring.

### Banking
AI is used for fraud detection, risk analysis and customer support.

### E-commerce
AI is used for product recommendations, personalized shopping and search systems.

### Education
AI can support personalized learning and automated assessment.

### Transportation
AI is used in navigation, traffic prediction and autonomous driving systems.

### Cybersecurity
AI can help detect suspicious activities and potential security threats.


## 6. Types of AI

### Narrow AI

Narrow AI is designed to perform a specific task.

Examples:
- Voice assistants
- Recommendation systems
- Spam detection systems

Most AI systems used today are Narrow AI.


### General AI

General AI refers to a hypothetical AI system that could perform a wide range of intellectual tasks at a human-like level.

It has not been achieved yet.


## 7. Benefits of AI

- Automation of repetitive tasks
- Faster decision making
- Improved efficiency
- Data analysis
- Personalized recommendations
- Reduced human error in some tasks


## 8. Challenges of AI

- Data privacy
- Bias in AI systems
- Security concerns
- High development cost
- Lack of transparency in some models
- Dependence on quality data


## 9. Key Takeaways

- AI is the broader concept of machine intelligence.
- Machine Learning is a subset of AI.
- Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning.
- AI is used in healthcare, banking, education, e-commerce and many other fields.
- Python is widely used for AI and Machine Learning development.
